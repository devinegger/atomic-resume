target_length: 400-500 words, five or six paragraphs
use_when: Career changes, pivots, employment gaps, roles where the resume alone won't make the case, or postings that ask you to address specific points.
rules:
  - Every claim must trace to an atom in profile/. No invented enthusiasm, no invented familiarity with the company.
  - Length is earned. If the extra paragraphs aren't doing work, use cover-letter-base.
  - Address the hard thing directly — the pivot, the gap, the missing credential. Reviewers notice it whether or not you mention it; mentioning it is how you control the framing.
  - Write in the user's voice, not a formal register they'd never use out loud.
  - Never open with "I am writing to apply for."
