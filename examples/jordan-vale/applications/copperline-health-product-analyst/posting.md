## Raw posting — verbatim

**Product Analyst**

Copperline Health builds scheduling and intake software for outpatient clinics.
We're looking for a Product Analyst to join our Care Access product team.

**What you'll do**

- Analyze product usage and clinic operations data to identify friction points
  in the scheduling and intake workflows
- Partner with product managers and clinic operations stakeholders to define
  requirements for new features
- Build and maintain reporting dashboards tracking key product and operational
  metrics
- Support root-cause analysis when clinics report billing or scheduling
  discrepancies
- Present findings to product and clinical operations leadership

**What we're looking for**

- 3+ years in an analyst role, ideally supporting a product or operations team
- Strong SQL skills; comfort with a modern BI tool (Tableau, Looker, or similar)
- Experience translating messy operational data into clear, decision-ready
  reporting
- Healthcare or clinical operations experience strongly preferred
- Comfortable presenting analysis to non-technical stakeholders, including
  clinical staff
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience

**Nice to have**

- Experience with billing, claims, or revenue-cycle data
- Familiarity with HIPAA-relevant data handling practices

**Compensation:** $78,000–$92,000
