Northwind Logistics — Annual Performance Review
Employee: Jordan Vale
Role: Senior Operations Analyst
Review period: January – December 2025
Reviewer: Regional Operations Director

OVERALL RATING: Exceeds Expectations

MANAGER COMMENTS

Jordan has had a strong year and has become the person the regional team goes
to when a question needs a defensible answer rather than an opinion.

The carrier scorecard work is the standout. Jordan built it from scratch in
2023 pulling from the TMS and the claims system, and it has since become the
backbone of our quarterly business reviews. Across the carriers we've reviewed
using it, on-time delivery has moved from 82% to 91% over three quarters. Two
carriers were renegotiated on the strength of it and one was dropped entirely.
That's a direct line from Jordan's analysis to a commercial decision, and it's
the kind of contribution that's rare at this level.

The freight billing audit was the other significant piece this year. Jordan
identified a pattern of accessorial overcharges going back roughly eighteen
months, worked through the backup documentation with the carriers, and we
recovered $184,000. Finance confirmed the figure. Jordan built the recurring
check that should stop it from happening again.

The invoice exception queue redesign has made a real difference to the AP
team's week, though I'd note we never formally measured it — the team's own
estimate is that reviews that used to sit for a couple of days now clear the
same morning. Worth being precise about that if it comes up anywhere formal.

The dispatch dashboard has had a mixed reception. It works and the regional
dispatch team uses it daily, but adoption outside that group hasn't happened
and I don't think we should count it as more than it is.

DEVELOPMENT AREAS

Jordan should get more comfortable presenting to senior audiences. The analysis
is consistently strong; the delivery undersells it. We've talked about Jordan
taking the QBR readout directly next quarter rather than preparing it for
someone else to present.

I'd also like to see Jordan build depth on the data side. The SQL is solid for
reporting and extracts but stops short of modeling, and that's the ceiling on
where this role can go.

EMPLOYEE COMMENTS

The scorecard was the thing I'm most pleased with, though what made it work
wasn't really the analysis — it was getting the carrier managers to agree on
what "on time" meant before we measured anything. That took most of the first
month and nobody would have noticed if I'd skipped it, but the whole thing
would have fallen apart in the first review.

I'd like to move toward work where I'm closer to the decisions rather than
producing the inputs for them.
